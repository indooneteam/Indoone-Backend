Indoone AI is the local AI core of the Indoone platform. It is designed to answer users through the Indoone backend without depending on a hosted AI provider.

Indoone improves through a controlled cycle: approved data is cleaned and versioned, a candidate model is trained, evaluation measures its quality, and only a candidate that passes promotion rules becomes active.

Conversation memory stores recent user and assistant messages for a conversation so the assistant can use previous turns as context. Memory storage is separate from model weights.

Knowledge retrieval lets Indoone look up relevant approved local documents before generating an answer. Retrieved knowledge is context for the model; it is not automatically written into model weights.

Sensitive user data and conversations should not be blindly added to training data. Any future training intake must use explicit approval, privacy controls, data-quality checks, evaluation, and rollback-safe model promotion.
